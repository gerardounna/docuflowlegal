Common Questions

How long does onboarding take?
Most firms are live with their first automations within 1–2 weeks. We handle the AI training — your team just reviews and approves the outputs.
What documents can DocuFlow automate?
NDAs, engagement letters, contracts, petitions, motions, leases, employment agreements, M&A documents — any standardized document with consistent structure. We don't do novel drafting; we automate the 80% of documents your firm produces repeatedly.
Is our data secure?
Your documents are used exclusively to train AI models for your firm and are never shared or used to benefit other clients. We comply with Texas Bar data handling requirements and can sign your firm's data processing agreement.
What if an AI makes a mistake?
Every AI draft goes to a human reviewer before it's finalized. DocuFlow doesn't replace attorney judgment — it handles the repetitive drafting so your team can focus on strategy and client service.
Do we need special software or integrations?
No. DocuFlow runs in your browser and connects to your document management system. We work with Word, Google Docs, Dropbox, Box and most major DMS platforms.
What if we need to cancel?
Month-to-month after the initial setup period. No annual contract required. You own your data and can export it at any time.
