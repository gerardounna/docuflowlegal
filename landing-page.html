Common Questions

How long does setup take?
Most firms are live within 1–2 weeks. The first week is document collection and AI training. The second week is configuration and testing. We don't launch until you approve the output.
What does "trained on our documents" mean?
You send us 10–20 representative documents. We use them to teach DocuFlow your firm's format, clause style, matter structure, and language patterns. The AI drafts in your voice — not generic legalese.
Do we need IT to implement this?
No. DocuFlow runs on our infrastructure. You don't install anything. Your team accesses it through a browser. Implementation is our job.
What if the AI makes a mistake?
Every document goes through attorney review before it leaves the firm. DocuFlow drafts — your team approves. The goal is to eliminate the first draft from scratch, not to remove human judgment.
Is our data secure?
Documents are used only to train your firm's AI instance and are not shared with other clients. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We can provide a data processing agreement (DPA) on request.
How does DocuFlow compare to Clio Draft?
Clio Draft is a template-based AI assistant that sits inside Clio. It charges $49 per attorney per month on top of your Clio subscription — and doesn't generate immigration petitions, employment contracts, or EEOC responses from scratch. DocuFlow trains on your firm's actual documents, handles Texas-specific practice areas, and charges a flat $497/month regardless of firm size. A 5-attorney firm pays $935/month for Clio Draft vs. $497/month for DocuFlow.
Does DocuFlow handle immigration documents — PERM, H-1B, family petitions?
Yes. Immigration is one of our strongest practice areas. We handle PERM labor certifications, H-1B support letters, I-130 family petitions, consular processing documents, and responsive immigration correspondence. Flat rate? Why does that matter for a growing firm?
Most legal software charges per attorney per month. Add two summer associates and your bill goes up — before the associates produce a single billable hour. DocuFlow charges $497/month flat. A firm of 3 attorneys or 15 attorneys pays the same. For growing Texas firms bringing on lateral hires or summer associates, that's a real cost advantage. No surprise invoices when your headcount changes.
