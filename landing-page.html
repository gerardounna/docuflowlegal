Common Questions

How long does setup take?
Most firms are live within 1–2 weeks. The first week is document collection and AI training. The second week is configuration and testing. We don't launch until you approve the output.
What does "trained on our documents" mean?
You send us 10–20 representative documents. We use them to teach DocuFlow your firm's format, clause style, matter structure, and language patterns. The AI drafts in your voice — not generic legalese.
Do we need IT to implement this?
No. DocuFlow runs on our infrastructure. You don't install anything. Your team accesses it through a browser. Implementation is our job.
What if the AI makes a mistake?
Every document goes through attorney review before it leaves the firm. DocuFlow drafts — your team approves. The goal is to eliminate the first draft from scratch, not to remove human judgment.
Who's liable if an AI-drafted document has an error?
You are. DocuFlow is a drafting tool — just like Word or a template library. The attorney reviews and approves every output before it goes to a client. We reduce drafting time; we don't change your ethical obligations. Your malpractice carrier covers you the same way it covers documents drafted with any software.
Is our data secure?
Documents are used only to train your firm's AI instance and are not shared with other clients. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We can provide a data processing agreement (DPA) on request.
What if we need to cancel?
Month-to-month after the initial setup period. No annual contract required. You own your data and can export it at any time.
What if the AI output doesn't meet our standards?
We don't launch until you've reviewed sample drafts from your actual documents and approved the quality. If the output doesn't meet your bar, you don't pay the setup fee. We're a partner in your workflow, not a vendor who disappears after onboarding.
What if we don't have existing documents to train the AI?
Most firms do — engagement letters, petitions, contracts, filings you already have. If you genuinely don't, we start from your practice area templates and industry-standard language, then refine as you use the system. Initial setup still takes 1–2 weeks.
How is this different from Clio or MyCase?
Clio and MyCase are practice management software — case files, billing, calendar. DocuFlow is document generation AI trained on your firm's own documents. We integrate with Clio but replace the time attorneys spend drafting from scratch. Different problem, different product.
We already use AI tools (AltaClaro, Clio AI, etc.). Why DocuFlow?
Many firms do — and that's exactly who we work best with. AltaClaro handles depositions and trial simulation. Clio AI handles billing entries. DocuFlow handles the specific document types your firm drafts repeatedly: severance agreements, EEOC responses, non-competes, visa petitions, I-9s. We complement existing tools — we're not trying to replace your entire stack. Several Am Law 200 firms have deployed AI firmwide in the last 90 days. We work with employment and immigration boutiques and mid-size firms where the ROI per document is clearest. Our flat $497/mo model often undercuts what firms pay in per-seat AI add-ons.
